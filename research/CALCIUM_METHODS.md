## 4. Explained variance / reverse EV

### EV formula as printed (two sources)
Source A, Tingley & Peyrache 2020 (Phil Trans R Soc B 375:20190231, local PDF rstb.2019.0231.pdf, page 2, Eq. 1; text: "EV ... usually referred to as explained variance (EV) [12]", "r_{A,B} being the cell-pair coherence between epochs A and B"). pdftotext garbles the glyphs; reconstructed from the extracted fragments, NOT visually checked (no PDF renderer here):
  EV = [ ( r_{W,POST} - r_{W,PRE} * r_{PRE,POST} ) / sqrt( (1 - r_{W,PRE}^2) (1 - r_{PRE,POST}^2) ) ]^2
Source B, Roscow et al. 2025 Nat Commun (PMC12644820, OA, Eq. 13, LaTeX read from XML, exact):
  EV = \left(\frac{ r_{TASK,POST} - r_{TASK,PRE} r_{POST,PRE} }{ \sqrt{(1 - r_{TASK,PRE}^{2})(1 - r_{POST,PRE}^{2})} }\right)^{2}
  Text: r = correlation coefficient between the PRE, TASK and POST cell-pair correlation matrices; "partial correlation between cell-pair coactivity during post-task ripples with that during the task, controlling for ... pre-task"; "REV was calculated by exchanging r_PRE and r_POST in eq. (13)".
Source C, Esteves 2026 (PMC12805373, Eq. 2) and Rusu 2024 (PMC10933476, Eq. 2) print the same with (Run, Rest1, Rest2) / (Task, R1, R2). Same algebra; squared partial correlation.
Kudrimoti 1999 / Tatsuno 2006 / Pennartz 2004 full texts are NOT available via Europe PMC or NCBI efetch (only ~7 KB metadata), so the original print of the formula could not be opened. Cell-pair correlation = Pearson on binned counts (stated in Esteves/Chang figure text; bin size per paper not checked).
